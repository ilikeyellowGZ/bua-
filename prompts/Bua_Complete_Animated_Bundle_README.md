## Important asset rule

The supplied screen and asset PNGs are immutable references. Thandi must remain the exact cream-and-navy mascot shown in those files. Extract approved sprites pixel-preservingly where possible; do not redraw, regenerate, trace, or substitute the mascot. Verify alpha after extraction and record any unresolved asset gap.
